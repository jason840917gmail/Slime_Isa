Controls

Left drag paints. Right or middle drag (or Space + drag) pans; wheel zooms. Alt + click picks the value under the cursor.
1–4 layers, B/R/F tools, [ ] brush size, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+S save, Del removes the selected marker.
Markers layer: click empty ground to drop the chosen marker, drag one to move it. A gate or cave connects the regions within two cells of it, plus its Leads to region.
