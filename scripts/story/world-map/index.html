Controls

Left drag paints. Right or middle drag (or Space + drag) pans; wheel zooms. Alt + click picks the value under the cursor.
1–4 layers, B/R/F/X tools, [ ] brush size, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+S save, Del removes the selected marker.
Markers layer: click empty ground to drop the chosen marker, drag one to move it. A gate or cave connects the regions within two cells of it, plus its Leads to region.
Swap chunks (X): drag block A, then drag a block of the same size (or click its north-west chunk); the two trade places with every layer, marker and passage on them. Esc cancels. A region that fills whole chunks moves or swaps from its inspector (Regions layer).
