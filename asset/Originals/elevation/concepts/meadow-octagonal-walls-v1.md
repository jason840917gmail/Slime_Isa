# Complete octagonal meadow wall sprites

## Generation convention

Generate complete, connected eight-sided formations as alignment previews and source sprites. Keep all eight directions assembled; do not separate or cut them into a tile atlas. Use the user's octagonal pit and raised-wall references for geometry, the bowed corner reference for concave corners, and existing meadow rock / Highland Green art for surfaces.

Every form includes its top grassy lip, wall face, and bottom termination. Show matching one-, two-, and three-level heights with repeated stone bands and no intermediate grass seams. Hollow centers and exterior background are transparent. Matching inner corners accompany the whole octagons.

These images visually demonstrate connectivity. Pixel-perfect joins and arbitrary-height reuse require inspection before runtime integration. No game elevation logic, media catalog, or authored scenes are modified.
