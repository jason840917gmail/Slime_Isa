# Side Sword Attack Spritesheet Prompt — 6 Frames

Attach the sword reference image before using this prompt.

```text
Using the attached sword reference, create one weapon-only side sword swing
spritesheet in modernized pixel-stylized top-down 2D game art. Preserve the
exact blade, guard, grip, pommel, colors, proportions, and sword identity.

Output one 384 x 64 PNG: a strict 6-column x 1-row grid of 64 x 64 cells.
Fill the entire sheet with flat, continuous solid chroma purple #8000FF. Cell
boundaries must be invisible: no visible grid lines, dividers, borders, gradients,
texture, checkerboard, labels, or scenery.

Author the sword facing right. The complete attack swings forward in front of
the attack origin, with the blade traveling through a clear horizontal arc:
1 compact ready pose, 2 sword drawn back in wind-up, 3 release and acceleration,
4 strongest forward swing/contact, 5 follow-through, 6 completed recovery.

Every frame must be the next moment in the same attack, not a separate pose.
Keep the pivot, scale, camera, lighting, sword design, and forward direction
consistent. Keep the full sword and slash effects inside each cell; effects are
strongest around frame 4 and fade by frame 6. Make the side row safe to mirror
for the opposite direction.

Weapon only: no character, UI, text, enemies, scenery, extra rows or columns,
cropped parts, duplicate frames, random redesigns, photorealism, glossy 3D, or
motion blur.
```
