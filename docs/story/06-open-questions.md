## Open after the dungeons were built (2026-10-17)

The owner approved building the three dungeons from their designs while he
was away; each was built with its designer's proposals, so these need only a
yes or a change. The designs: [Fatty's Lair](../design/2026-10-16-dungeon-fattys-lair.md),
[the Weaver Hollow](../design/2026-10-16-dungeon-weaver-hollow.md),
[the Crystal Mine](../design/2026-10-16-dungeon-crystal-caverns.md); how they
are built: [DUNGEONS.md](../godot/DUNGEONS.md).

137. **Fatty's Lair, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the way out is a plank barricade a Heavy Roll breaks
     (the heavy plate gets its first use on the walk home with Pearl 1);
     Burr's cell has two keyholes and his winch opens the Deep; he waits in
     daylight at the bolt-hole by the Verdant Gate and says the town must see
     the Pearl; Fatty's second phase throws clods and sends a mud ripple a Roll
     passes; the Glow-moss Burrow's heart quarter (a Lantern come-back) is one
     of Slimeshire's four; an armour statue in a dungeon is always a heavy
     block (no decorative ones); the outer gate stays up once the inner winch
     turns.
138. **The Weaver Hollow, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the Hollow is the Watch Oak, an old Thorn Guard watch
     fort inside a giant oak; the Matron's cocoon opens for about 4 s after a
     blast and re-spins in about 1.5 s, three rounds; a blast also stuns the
     Brood Warden; blast kegs only in the Pillar room; the Cocoon Mimic
     swallowed small key 2; floor webs are painted; the way out drops into the
     forecourt; no decorative cocoons or egg sacs in the Hollow or Tom's maze;
     if it plays long, the Pillar room goes first. Not built yet: Mossy's tip
     at the forecourt, and quest 6's text still tells the old nest fight.
139. **The Crystal Mine, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the name "the Crystal Mine"; the Foreman's Gate takes
     two keys; the mine cart is a fade, not a ride; the Shardback's slam puts
     braziers out; the Colossus's arena is one screen; his weak spot opens on
     one side while all four braziers burn, and his stomps put them out; the
     mine introduces the Bone Leaper; a slime that reaches the Chasm without
     the Bow is sent to Burr by the smith; the exploration message shows on
     stepping into daylight at the Lookout; the mine is costed for 100 energy.
140. **Behind a diamond hill.** Since the side walls became the only look,
     pressing straight down behind a diamond hill's back face stops at its tip
     (walking along the face gets past). It comes from how the approved side
     walls start at back corners.
     *Proposal:* keep it; diamond hills are rare in the game's worlds.
