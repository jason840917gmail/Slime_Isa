## Open after the dungeons were built (2026-10-17)

The owner approved building the three dungeons from their designs while he
was away; each was built with its designer's proposals, so these need only a
yes or a change. The designs: [Fatty's Lair](../design/2026-10-16-dungeon-fattys-lair.md),
[the Weaver Hollow](../design/2026-10-16-dungeon-weaver-hollow.md),
[the Crystal Mine](../design/2026-10-16-dungeon-crystal-caverns.md); how they
are built: [DUNGEONS.md](../godot/DUNGEONS.md).

137. **Fatty's Lair, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the way out is a plank barricade a Heavy Roll breaks
     (the heavy plate gets its first use on the walk home with Pearl 1);
     Burr's cell has two keyholes and his winch opens the Deep; he waits in
     daylight at the bolt-hole by the Verdant Gate and says the town must see
     the Pearl; Fatty's second phase throws clods and sends a mud ripple a Roll
     passes; the Glow-moss Burrow's heart quarter (a Lantern come-back) is one
     of Slimeshire's four; an armour statue in a dungeon is always a heavy
     block (no decorative ones).
     *Superseded in part (2026-10-17):* the Pearl now lies inside, so the heavy
     plate is first used in the second half; the rework is question 142.
     *Since then:* the Lair caves in once Fatty falls, and its come-back secrets, the Glow-moss
     Burrow among them, moved outside into Slimeshire (owner, 2026-10-19); the Verdant Gate
     becomes a fallen tree (owner, 2026-11-17), and Fatty's fight changes with the boss pass
     (question 166).
138. **The Weaver Hollow, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the Hollow is the Watch Oak, an old Thorn Guard watch
     fort inside a giant oak; the Matron's cocoon opens for about 4 s after a
     blast and re-spins in about 1.5 s, three rounds; a blast also stuns the
     Brood Warden; blast kegs only in the Pillar room; the Cocoon Mimic
     swallowed small key 2; floor webs are painted; the way out drops into the
     forecourt; no decorative cocoons or egg sacs in the Hollow or Tom's maze;
     if it plays long, the Pillar room goes first. Not built yet: Mossy's tip
     at the forecourt, and quest 6's text still tells the old nest fight.
     *Superseded in part (2026-10-17):* the Matron's rounds 2 and 3 and the
     Pearl's place changed; the rework is question 143.
139. **The Crystal Mine, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the name "the Crystal Mine"; the Foreman's Gate takes
     two keys; the mine cart is a fade, not a ride; the Shardback's slam puts
     braziers out; the Colossus's arena is one screen; his weak spot opens on
     one side while all four braziers burn, and his stomps put them out; the
     mine introduces the Bone Leaper; a slime that reaches the Chasm without
     the Bow is sent to Burr by the smith; the exploration message shows on
     stepping into daylight at the Lookout; the mine is costed for 100 energy.
     *Superseded in part (2026-10-17):* the Pearl now lies inside, and the
     Colossus's fight gained a climb on his wall (the Feral exam); the rework is
     question 144. *Since 2026-11-17 Burr lives in Slimeshire's C4 (question 150).*
140. **Behind a diamond hill.** Since the side walls became the only look,
     pressing straight down behind a diamond hill's back face stops at its tip
     (walking along the face gets past). It comes from how the approved side
     walls start at back corners.
     *Proposal:* keep it; diamond hills are rare in the game's worlds.

## Open after the Pearl moved inside (2026-10-17)

The owner adopted the Pearl inside the dungeon, before its boss (the
[decisions archive](07-decisions-archive.md), 2026-10-17), and had the three
dungeons reworked for it while he was away. Each was built with the
[plan's proposals](../design/2026-10-17-pearl-mid-dungeon.md#open-points), so
these need only a yes or a change. How they are built:
[DUNGEONS.md](../godot/DUNGEONS.md#the-seal); the skins:
[SKINS.md](../godot/SKINS.md).

141. **The seal and the take, as built.**
     *Proposal (built):* the mini-boss guards the Pearl in all three; the big
     key stays for the boss door; the seal is one kit piece, a shutter bound by
     a glowing ring in the boss's colour, and the take shows a 1.5 s glimpse of
     the entrance's seal slamming; while sealed the boss roars far off every 45
     to 60 s (presentation only); each boss leaves a full heart (max energy up);
     the Colossus's old "enraged" phase is now his second phase
     (`phaseTwoShare`), so it never mixes with the Pearl's rage.
     *Not built:* the music's drums while sealed and the journal's Pearl slot.
     *Open:* a seal in a doorway at a world's south edge (the Lair's R1, the
     Mine's M1) lies half under the HUD bar, in the glimpse too; the roar's
     line shows over the skin's line on the take.
142. **Fatty's Lair, reworked.**
     *Proposal (built):* the Pearl in R12, the Larder, behind the Big Squint;
     door 3 opens without a key; the Heavy second half: the Larder's plank
     barricade, the new Weigh House (C2), R10's statue on a plate and R9's big
     chest by a plate; the hub's drawbridge (since 2026-10-19: two plates,
     the slime on one and the freed Burr on the other, no Heavy skin);
     seals in R1's south doorway and R13's north doorway; Fatty's second phase
     is answered by a Heavy Roll that topples him. The Lair's mouth moved to a
     hill in D5, behind a maze and a bridge of falling slabs (the owner's ask).
     *Not built:* Fatty shrinking into a small calm slime (his death clip
     plays).
     *Since 2026-10-24:* in his second phase a thrown mud ball throws him off balance for 2 s, a
     Heavy Roll then knocks him down for 3 s, and the Spear hits his eye. It changes again with
     the boss pass (question 166).
143. **The Weaver Hollow, reworked.**
     *Proposal (built):* the Pearl in the Hoard, guarded by the Brood Warden;
     the Silk second half: the Hoard stair's web wall, C1 webbed, the new C4
     Spinning Loft, small key 3 in a silk parcel; B4 and B6 an optional wing;
     the Matron's round 2 is a cage of web walls a thrown bomb cannot pass, and
     round 3 a webbed floor the Roll crosses; her spit no longer roots; seals in
     T1 and C2.
     *Open:* the Slime Spiders' web shots are the forest's, untuned for C4.
     *As built (2026-11-14):* its root gate needs the Goo Bomb, which nobody teaches yet, so the
     Hollow cannot be entered in play.
144. **The Crystal Mine, reworked.**
     *Proposal (built):* the Pearl in M13, the Den, behind the Shardback; the
     Chasm is the first half's last lock; the Feral second half: the Den's root
     wall, M11's rift, M12's balcony (with the big key), the shaft's roots; M10
     and M11's veil are an optional Lantern wing; the braziers' weak spot stays,
     and after three stomps he climbs the north wall, where the slime climbs
     beside him and three Spear hits on his claws stun him 2.5 s with his back
     open; seals at M1's mouth and at the climb-out.
     *Open:* the Bow is not built, so the Chasm (and the Pearl after it) cannot
     be reached in play yet; `?sealed` or `room=` reach them.
145. **The Feral skin's details.**
     *Proposal (built):* it climbs up and down a face (not along it), strikes
     while it clings, and a hit knocks it off; the Spear's chain runs at half
     its times, its damage and the Lunge unchanged.
     *Superseded (2026-11-12 and 2026-11-17):* a held interact clings to painted vines and the
     movement keys move the slime over them, in every direction, as in the playground
     ([SKINS.md](../godot/SKINS.md#feral)); the Spear's chain went with the shapes (question 146),
     so what the skin changes in a move is question 157.

## Open after the owner's answers (2026-11-17)

From the owner's answers to questions 146 to 156 and his ideas of the same day (the
[decisions archive](07-decisions-archive.md#decisions-by-date), 2026-11-17).

157. **The Feral skin's change to a move.** Each skin has its gate and a change to a move the
     slime already has; Feral's was "the Spear's attacks hit twice as fast" (question 56), and the
     Spear is now one ability with no chain.
     *Proposal:* the Gulp works while the slime clings: it sucks and throws from the wall (built
     already in throw mode), so a climber can fight.
158. **Feral's vines and the War Axe's.** Question 86 kept them apart by look: Feral's walls showed
     thick roots and rocky handholds, and the War Axe's lock was a curtain of leafy vines hanging
     in front of an opening. Feral now climbs vines.
     *Proposal:* Feral's vines grow flat over the rock of a cliff's face, and the War Axe's leafy
     curtain always hangs loose in front of an opening, never on a face the slime climbs.
159. **Glimmerhill's parts.** Pearl 1 wakes Market Hill. Which part does each next Pearl wake,
     what waits for Pearls 4 to 8, and what does a woken part bring?
     *Proposal:* Pearl 2 wakes West Hill (A4: the family's home, the tailor), Pearl 3 the south
     gate's quarter (B5: Bram and Zing's throwing booth, Release 1's game); the farms and the lake
     (A5), the secret underground and certain buildings wait for Pearls 4 to 8. Every way into town,
     the Wilds' openings into A4 and B5 included, waits for the Heavy Roll, like Market Hill's tree.
     Pearl 2 still lights Slimeshire's Forge. A part's art is first darkened and greyed by a shader,
     with dry and running fountains and a sleeping pose; new art only where the shader falls short.
160. **Release 1's ability upgrades.** The Charged Roll is the first (built as `roll-charge`; it
     also opens C5's island bridge). Which others, and where is each found? The review proposed a
     Spin Roll (the War Axe's Spin) and a Quake Bomb (the Hammer's Quake), but those strikes now
     belong to the War Axe and Hammer abilities.
     *Proposal:* one upgrade per chapter on a power Release 1 teaches (the Roll, the Goo Bomb, the
     Lantern, the Bow), each found whole in a secret place, never on the main path.
161. **Potion vessels.** The owner's "recipients" for potions.
     *Proposal:* a potion needs a flask; drinking it gives the flask back; how many flasks the
     slime owns is how many potions it can carry; flasks come from Glimmerhill's shop and a few
     secrets. The craft panel opens at a campfire, lists the known recipes and fills a flask.
162. **The liftables' weight cue.** The owner's idea: a coloured border by weight (green, yellow,
     red), "or a better option".
     *Proposal:* the weight shows in the thing itself (pots and jars light, crates and barrels
     medium, boulders and iron heavy); only the thing in the Gulp's reach is highlighted, with one
     to three pips; a "too heavy" pip and a strain wobble show when the Gulp's level is too low.
     Borders on everything clutter every screen, and green, yellow and red are the colours
     colour-blind players mix up most. Release 1 has only level 1, so its prompt alone does the job.
163. **The Verdant Gate's fallen tree.** What does the Postern chest hold instead of the green key,
     and does the place keep the name "the Verdant Gate"?
     *Proposal:* the place keeps its name, its gate long gone and a grey fallen tree across its
     road; the Postern chest holds coins and a potion.
164. **The Wilds' ways into Gloop Forest and the Crystal Caverns** (question 87). The north links
     into Slimeshire and Gloop Forest were closed on 2026-10-31 (owner: "we can create something
     with the lash or a bridge to the left of the region later"), and the others are plain
     openings, with no web maze or climb.
     *Proposal:* the South Wilds' opening into Gloop Forest (E6 to E5) becomes the web maze only
     the Silk skin passes, and the North Wilds' opening into the Caverns (H3 to H4) the climb only
     the Feral skin makes, as question 87 had them.
165. **Lily or Dora.** Question 7 renamed Lily the Fishergirl to Dora, but the game still says
     Lily, and a new Glimmerhill villager, Gramps Reel, names her.
     *Proposal:* Dora, as decided.
166. **How throws hurt each boss.** Decided when the bosses are revisited, Fatty first. Today a
     thrown mud ball throws Fatty off balance, a Heavy Roll knocks him down, and the Spear hits his
     eye; the Matron opens her cocoon to a blast, and the Colossus bares his weak spot to the
     braziers and the Spear.
167. **Who teaches the War Axe and the Hammer, and when.** They are abilities now, learned from
     NPCs or found in secrets. Until now the War Axe waited for the next release, its teacher open
     (question 33), and the smith taught the Hammer at the Workshop in Chapter 5 or 6
     (question 51).
