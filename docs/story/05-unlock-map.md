# Unlock Map

Every **permanent** item in the game: where it is, how the slime earns it, and
what it opens. Consumables (potions, materials) are not listed. This table is
the checklist for placing secrets and for the item ceremony: every row with
"full" in the Ceremony column gets the full item-get moment the first time.

The slime's powers come from three places ([Direction](01-direction.md#the-slimes-powers)):
**skins** from the Pearls, and **shapes** and **abilities** from NPCs and old
manuscripts. In Release 1 NPCs teach and chests hold the finds; manuscript
pieces come later (owner, 2026-10-15). Shapes replace the crafted weapons and
tools below, which go with the old system once the three skins are built
(question 48). The Spear, the War Axe and the Hammer are built in the rework
mode, which lends all three for now. The Spear comes from Old Veteran Yoi
(question 50) and the Hammer after Release 1 (question 51). The War Axe waits
for the next release (owner, 2026-10-16, question 94), and who teaches it is
Open (question 33). Release 1 has eight powers besides the Gulp, about three
a chapter: the Spear, the Roll and the Heavy skin (Chapter 1); the Goo Bomb
and the Silk skin (Chapter 2); the Lantern, the Feral skin and the Bow
(Chapter 3) (question 94). Many items below are quest rewards today, and
quests no longer make sense: in their place an NPC hands over or teaches its
find when the slime talks to it, and the other finds lie in chests, each with
its ceremony (owner, 2026-10-16, question 60). *Changed 2026-10-16 (sixth
round): Yoi teaches the Spear in the
[prologue](../design/2026-10-16-prologue.md) (question 97), Tom the Goo Bomb
(question 95), and Burr builds the Bow from the Matron's thread (questions 102
and 106); the Spear is Release 1's only shape (question 96). Release 1 has
whole manuscripts, each found in a chest as one find, and pieces come later
(question 65); what they teach is question 108 (Open).* *Changed 2026-10-16
(seventh round, question 108): they teach campfire recipes and lore, so every
Release 1 power comes from an NPC or a Pearl; manuscripts that teach powers
start in the next release.* *Changed 2026-11-17 (owner, question 146): "shape"
is dropped as a concept. The slime has two kinds of power: skins, from the
Pearls, and abilities, from NPCs or found in secrets; the Spear, the War Axe and
the Hammer are abilities. The Gulp's throw is the fight, and there is no basic
attack. Abilities improve through upgrades found whole in secret places, each
with its ceremony, and the belt's four slots are secrets too.*

## Health and energy totals

Max HP is 100 at the start, with no hearts. A heart quarter adds nothing on
its own: the fourth quarter completes a heart, and each whole heart adds +20
(owner, 2026-10-08). Quarters come four to a map, so the totals below count
whole hearts. Each boss also gives a full heart (+20) on top of its region's
four quarters (owner, 2026-10-16, question 17), and the small Gloop Cavern
holds one of Gloop Forest's four, with none of its own (question 18).

| As built (2026-11-14), with every placed Release 1 quarter (seven: four in Slimeshire, three in Gloop Forest and the Weaver Hollow) and the three boss hearts | 180 |
