# Unlock Map

Every **permanent** item in the game: where it is, how the slime earns it, and
what it opens. Consumables (potions, materials) are not listed. This table is
the checklist for placing secrets and for the item ceremony: every row with
"full" in the Ceremony column gets the full item-get moment the first time.

The slime's powers come from three places ([Direction](01-direction.md#the-slimes-powers)):
**skins** from the Pearls, and **shapes** and **abilities** from NPCs and old
manuscripts. Shapes replace the crafted weapons and tools below: the Spear,
the War Axe and the Hammer are built in the rework mode, which lends all three
for now; who teaches each is Open.

Changes of 2026-10-15 ([Direction](01-direction.md#history)): the Pearls grant
skins (Heavy, Silk, ...), not abilities. Shapes replace crafted weapons.
Swallow and essences are marked for deletion, so essences are not listed. The
Bomb breaks cracked ground. Squash Slam stays; where it is learned is Open.

Since 2026-10-09 every active ability plays the ceremony the first time it is
learned (roadmap 4.13), and so do the first of each weapon, tool and key and every
Goo Heart. Bags, heart quarters, the lift upgrades and story items will get it
when they are built.

## Health and energy totals

Max HP is 100 at the start, with no hearts. A heart quarter adds nothing on
its own: the fourth quarter completes a heart, and each whole heart adds +20
(owner, 2026-10-08). Quarters come four to a map, so the totals below count
whole hearts.

| By the end of Chapter 2 | Max HP |
|---|---|
| Quarters only, Gloop Cavern sharing Gloop Forest's four (8 quarters) | 140 |
| Quarters only, Gloop Cavern counting as its own map (12 quarters) | 160 |
| Either of those plus two boss hearts (Proposal) | 180 or 200 |
| Today, for comparison (5 Goo Hearts of +10) | 150 |

The Matron's damage (18 to 30 a hit) was tuned for 100–150 HP. Recheck it once
the totals are decided.
