# Chapter 3 — Crystal Caverns (sketch)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Region** | Crystal Caverns (`crystal-caverns` exists; its exit from Gloop Forest is locked: "sealed with crystal") |
| **Goal** | Light the dark, free the smith, rebuild the Workshop, and break the Colossus |
| **Boss** | Crystal Colossus (Idea: armored, weak to hammers, teaches weapon switching) |
| **Earned** | Glow Armor, the Workshop and the Slam Hammer; Proposal: Pearl 3 with an Open ability, and a boss heart |
| **Status** | Sketch. Everything here is an Idea or a Proposal. |

This sketch ties together pieces that already wait in the roadmap's parking
lot: the Crystal Colossus, the Glow form, and the Workshop with its first
recipe, the Slam Hammer, which is built and parked. Together they make one
chapter.

## Beats (Proposal)

1. **The seal breaks.** After the Matron falls, the crystal sealing the
   caverns cracks. Mossy is already inside: "It is dark in here. Really dark."
2. **The first dungeon.** The caverns are a dark maze. Early on, the slime
   finds the **Glow** material and gulps it, learning the **Glow Armor**
   (Idea in the roadmap: "Glow (crystal shard) lights dark caves"). From then
   on the slime lights its own way, and the Glow costs energy over time.
3. **The smith.** Deep in the maze the slime finds Pip's dad, trapped in
   crystal. He came here years ago looking for metal to fight the worms. The
   slime frees him, and he goes home to Slimeshire. This is a big moment for
   Pip and a visible change in town.
4. **The Workshop** [`the-old-workshop`] (Built, parked). The smith asks for
   the Workshop to be rebuilt. The restoration should take iron bars by then,
   as the roadmap says. The Workshop's first recipe is the **Slam Hammer**,
   made by hammering at the Workshop and ending in the full ceremony.
5. **The Crystal Colossus.** A golem of crystal grown around Pearl 3. Spears
   glance off its armor, and the Slam Hammer cracks it. The fight teaches
   weapon switching: crack the armor with the hammer, then switch to the spear
   on the soft core.
6. **Pearl 3.** It grants its unique ability permanently; which ability is
   Open. Plop tells the second piece of the story, and Gorge's rumbling grows
   louder.
