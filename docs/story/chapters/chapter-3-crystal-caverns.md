# Chapter 3 — Crystal Caverns (sketch)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Region** | Crystal Caverns (`crystal-caverns` exists; its exit from Gloop Forest is locked: "sealed with crystal") |
| **Goal** | Light the dark, free the smith, rebuild the Workshop, and break the Colossus |
| **Boss** | Crystal Colossus (Idea: armored, weak to the hammer, teaches switching shapes) |
| **Earned** | The Lantern (if not earned before), the Workshop and the hammer shape; Proposal: Pearl 3 with an Open skin, and a boss heart |
| **Status** | Sketch. Everything here is an Idea or a Proposal. |

This sketch ties together pieces that already wait in the roadmap's parking
lot: the Crystal Colossus, light in the dark, and the Workshop with the hammer
(today its first recipe, the Slam Hammer, built and parked; a shape in the
plan). Together they make one chapter.

## Beats (Proposal)

1. **The seal breaks.** After the Matron falls, the crystal sealing the
   caverns cracks. Mossy is already inside: "It is dark in here. Really dark."
2. **The first dungeon.** The caverns are a dark maze. Early on, the slime
   finds the **Lantern** (an ability, built in the rework mode): an ember
   lights the slime in dark rooms, and a short flame lights candles and burns
   dry hedges. Where it is learned is Open; it could also be Chapter 2's key
   tool. The Glow form, gulped from a crystal shard, went with the gulped
   forms on 2026-10-15.
3. **The smith.** Deep in the maze the slime finds Pip's dad, trapped in
   crystal. He came here years ago looking for metal to fight the worms. The
   slime frees him, and he goes home to Slimeshire. This is a big moment for
   Pip and a visible change in town.
4. **The Workshop** [`the-old-workshop`] (Built, parked). The smith asks for
   the Workshop to be rebuilt. The restoration should take iron bars by then,
   as the roadmap says. The smith teaches the **hammer shape** there (today
   the Slam Hammer recipe), ending in the full ceremony.
5. **The Crystal Colossus.** A golem of crystal grown around Pearl 3. Spears
   glance off its armor, and the hammer cracks it. The fight teaches
   switching shapes: crack the armor with the hammer, then switch to the spear
   on the soft core.
6. **Pearl 3.** It grants its skin permanently; which skin is Open. Plop
   tells the second piece of the story, and Gorge's rumbling grows louder.
