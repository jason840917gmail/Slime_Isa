This sketch ties together pieces that already waited in the roadmap's parking
lot: the Crystal Colossus, light in the dark, and the smith. Since 2026-10-15
the chapter is part of Release 1, while the smith teaches the Hammer in
Chapter 5 or 6 (question 51). On 2026-10-16 the owner set the rest
([questions 56, 58 and 59](../06-open-questions.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15)):

- the smith runs the crystal mine, which the Colossus has blocked, and gives
  the Lantern;
- the Colossus falls to his braziers, not to the Hammer;
- Pearl 3 grants the Feral skin. It was to grant the Swim skin (owner,
  2026-10-15), but the dark, dry caverns do not fit it.

Later the same day he answered more
([questions 68, 69 and 81](../06-open-questions.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15)):
a climbable wall has vines and irregularities, the Colossus climbs out of
reach and throws minions down, and a web-wall maze that only the Silk skin
passes leads into the caverns.

In the fourth and fifth rounds the same day
([questions 84 to 94](../06-open-questions.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15))
the Bow joined the chapter, the War Axe left Release 1, climbable walls got
thick roots and rocky handholds, and a hill from the Wilds that only the
Feral skin climbs became a second way in.

In the sixth round the same day
([questions 95 to 106](../06-open-questions.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15))
Burr's Bow came to need the Matron's thread, the chapter's main path got one
Bow lock, and the Spear stayed Release 1's only shape.

## Beats

1. **Burr's Bow** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, questions 85 and 94). When the
   slime comes back to the West village after Chapter 2, Burr, freed from
   Fatty's Lair in Chapter 1, gives his thanks: the **Bow**, with the full
   ceremony. It is an Ability Box power, and each arrow costs energy. Its
   locks are far targets in a straight line across gaps and water, far
   enemies that cannot be reached otherwise, and enemies that open doors when
   they die (question 76).
   - *Until 2026-10-16 (fifth round): Burr gave it right after Fatty's Lair,
     in Chapter 1 (question 80).*
   - *Changed 2026-10-16 (sixth round, questions 102 and 106):* visited
     before Pearl 2, Burr thanks the slime and gives a tip: "I am making you
     something, come back when the forest is free." He needs silk and other
     things to build the Bow. The silk is the **Matron's thread**, a spool the
     slime finds in her nest after she falls (with the Silk skin it can take
     it from her web); the other things are already in his workshop. With the
     thread and Pearl 2 home, he teaches the Bow: one find, one trip back, and
     no crafting.
   - The chapter's main path uses the Bow at least once: a far eye switch
     across the mine's chasm (beat 4). The Colossus does not need it
     (question 102).
2. **The web-wall maze** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 81). A maze of
   web walls on Gloop Forest's east side, which only the Silk skin passes,
   leads into the caverns: the Silk skin opens Chapter 3, as a skin opens the
   next region. Proposal: Mossy is already inside: "It is dark in here.
   Really dark."
   - *Until 2026-10-16 (Proposal): after the Matron fell, the crystal sealing
     the caverns cracked.*
3. **The first dark rooms** (Proposal). The caverns are a dark maze. The Glow
   form, gulped from a crystal shard, went with the gulped forms on
   2026-10-15.
4. **The smith's mine camp** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 59). The
   smith, Pip's dad, runs the crystal mine, which the Crystal Colossus has
   blocked. He is not trapped, and there is no rescue: the slime finds him
   safe at his mine camp.
   - He gives the slime the **Lantern** (an ability, built in the rework
     mode), the Forge's ember, with the full ceremony: an ember lights the
     slime in dark rooms, and a short flame lights candles and braziers and
     burns dry plant growth (deadthorn, hay, dry roots, old banners) and the
     wood a Heavy Roll knocked down (owner, 2026-10-16, question 93).
   - With it the slime crosses the dark maze to the Colossus.
   - **The mine's chasm** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 102): on the
     main path, a far eye switch across the chasm opens the way with an arrow
     from the Bow.
   - Rotten mine timbers in the way are the Heavy Roll's, not the Lantern's
     (owner, 2026-10-16, question 93).
   - Pip's story stays relighting the Forge (Chapter 2); it is no longer
     about finding his dad.
   - *Until 2026-10-16 (Proposal): the smith was trapped in crystal, deep in
     the maze, where he had come looking for metal to fight the worms, and
     the slime freed him.*
5. **The Workshop** [`the-old-workshop`] (Built, parked). The smith teaches
   the **Hammer** shape there, in Chapter 5 or 6, after Release 1 (owner,
   2026-10-15, question 51), and Release 1 has no iron bars (no harvesting).
   What the Workshop does in Release 1 is Open (question 51).
6. **The Crystal Colossus** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 59). A golem
   of crystal grown around Pearl 3.
   - Four braziers ring his arena. Only when all four burn, lit by the
     Lantern, is a weak spot on his armour bared, at a random place, for the
     Spear or the Axe. *Changed 2026-10-16 (owner, question 94): for the
     Spear alone, as the War Axe waits for the next release. Release 1 with
     one shape is question 96.* *Answered 2026-10-16 (sixth round): the Spear
     is Release 1's only shape (question 96), and the Colossus does not need
     the Bow (question 102).*
   - His stomps blow the braziers out, one at a time.
   - **Planned (owner, 2026-10-16, question 69):** the Pearl in his chest
     made him part beast. Between stomps he climbs his arena's walls on
     crystal claws, so the Pearl's power shows in the fight. Out of reach, he
     throws some of his minions down; killed, they can drop energy shards
     (the boss-chamber rule of question 40).
   - *Until 2026-10-16 (Idea): spears glanced off his armour, the hammer
     cracked it, and the fight taught switching shapes.*
7. **Pearl 3** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 56). It grants the
   **Feral** skin permanently:
   - Gate: the slime climbs some walls it could not climb before, which
     opens the world. Claws show while it climbs. To climb, the player holds
     W (interact); letting go midway drops the slime. A climbable wall looks
     different from the others: it has vines and irregularities. The slime
     climbs up and down, and a fall costs nothing (owner, 2026-10-16,
     question 68). Vines are also the War Axe's lock (question 86).
     *Changed 2026-10-16 (owner, questions 86 and 91): a climbable wall shows
     thick roots and rocky handholds on the cliff face, and the Axe's vines
     are leafy curtains hanging in front of an opening. A climbable wall never
     stands beside a heavy block; held W next to both does whichever the
     slime faces.*
   - From the Wilds, a hill that only the Feral skin climbs leads into the
     caverns, so that way opens once Feral is won (owner, 2026-10-16,
     question 87).
   - Change to a move: the Spear's attacks hit twice as fast.
   - The ideas offered before (Prism, Burrow, Echo and Shard) are declined.
     The Swim skin moves to a later Pearl, and the deep-water secrets are
     come-back secrets for it.
   - Proposal: Plop tells the second piece of the story, and Gorge's rumbling
     grows louder.
8. **Home, and the open world** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 58).
   Once the Colossus falls, the smith goes home to Slimeshire. Release 1's
   content ends here, but the game goes on: the player explores the first
   three chapters' regions, the West village and the Wilds that connect them,
   using the new powers, and the game says that from here on it is
   exploration and that more areas come in later releases. Barriers in the
   world close the ways to the regions that are not in Release 1 (a fallen
   bridge, a landslide, a Thorn Guard post), and each says that more lies
   beyond (owner, 2026-10-16, question 73).
