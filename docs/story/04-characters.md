# Characters

Built descriptions come from `src/game/content/npcs/NpcDefinitions.ts` and the
quest files. Everything about where a character is going is a Proposal.

## The hero

**The slime.** A newborn from the Wellspring's last drop (Proposal), and a
silent hero. It learns everything from the townsfolk, and its gift is the Gulp.
Its name is Open. See [Premise](./02-premise.md#the-slime).

## Slimeshire

### Elder Plop (Built)

- **Who:** a patient elder who watches over the village path. "Keep your goo
  bright and your eyes open. The forest has grown restless lately."
- **Role:** the mentor and Chapter 1's main quest giver. In Chapter 2 Plop asks
  to see real iron gear.
- **Quests:**
  - A Place to Work. It already teaches the stone tool recipes; Planned: Plop
    also hands over the first bag here.
  - Stone Tools, which teaches Dodge.
  - The One-Eyed Guardian.
  - Iron Gear.
  - Proposal: A Home of Your Own, the house quest with the vault.
- **Proposal:**
  - The only one old enough to remember Gorge and the shattering. Plop tells the
    story a piece at a time, one piece per Pearl returned.
  - Plop is the first to see the slime born and calls it a sign.

### Mossy (Built)

- **Who:** a purple scout, always a step ahead. "Psst! Over here. Keep your
  voice down."
- **Role:** the main quest giver at the front line. In Chapter 1 Mossy gives
  Worm Trouble, which teaches Jump. In Chapter 2 Mossy moves to the forest camp
  and gives Beyond the Verdant Gate (Stretch Lash), A Harder Pick, and The
  Matron's Nest.
- **Proposal:** the recurring scout. Mossy arrives in every new region first,
  sets up a camp, and is waiting there when the slime arrives. This is a little
  like the owl in Zelda. Mossy tracks the Pearls' trail toward Volcano Ridge.

### Pip (Built)

- **Who:** the smith's son, who has wanted to light the Forge again since the
  worms came. "My dad was Slimeshire's smith, before the worms chased him off."
- **Role:** Rekindle the Forge (Chapter 2).
- **Proposal:** his arc is finding his dad. The smith is found in Chapter 3
  (see below). Pip grows from a kid with a dream into Slimeshire's new smith.

### Lili (Built)

- **Who:** the friendliest slime in the clearing. "There is always room for one
  more friend in our clearing."
- **Role:** A Tonic for Lili, the optional quest that teaches the healing
  tonic.
- **Proposal:** the town's heart. Lili notices every change in Slimeshire as
  Pearls return and welcomes each slime who comes back.

### Lily the Fishergirl (Built)

- **Who:** a patient fisher at the lake. "The big one always bites right after
  you give up."
- **Role:** Snack for the Road (optional).
- **Open:** the names **Lili** and **Lily** are too close for players to tell
  apart. One of them should be renamed.

### Sunny (Built)

- **Who:** sunshine in slime form. "A little sunshine makes every adventure
  brighter!"
- **Role:** came to the forest with Mossy to pick berries. Gives Sunny's Basket
  in Chapter 2 (optional, uses the Stretch Lash).

### The smith, Pip's dad (Proposal)

- Chased off by the worms. In Chapter 3 the slime finds him trapped by crystal
  in the caverns, where he went looking for metal to fight back with.
- Rescuing him leads to rebuilding the **Workshop**, which is already built and
  parked until after Chapter 2. The Workshop's first recipe, the Slam Hammer,
  is the weapon the Crystal Colossus is weak to.

### The worm who surrenders (Idea)

From the roadmap's parking lot: a named worm who gives up at low HP. If the
slime spares him, he becomes Slimeshire's shopkeeper, which also gives coins a
use. **Proposal:** he is the first sign that the worms were refugees, not
villains.
