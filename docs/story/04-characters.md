# Characters

Built descriptions come from `godot/game/data/npc-definitions.json` and the
quest files. Everything about where a character is going is a Proposal.

## The hero

**The slime.** A newborn from the Wellspring's last drop (Proposal), and a
silent hero. The townsfolk and old manuscripts teach it shapes and abilities,
and each Pearl grants a skin. In Release 1 the townsfolk teach and chests hold
the finds; manuscript pieces come later (owner, 2026-10-15). Its Gulp grabs
and throws. Its name is Open. *Since 2026-10-16 (Planned, question 97): its
birth is the [prologue](../design/2026-10-16-prologue.md)'s first scene, and
Yoi, the veteran it saves there, is its first teacher.* *Changed 2026-10-16
(seventh round, question 108): Release 1's whole manuscripts teach campfire
recipes and lore, not powers; manuscripts that teach powers start in the
next release.*
See [Premise](02-premise.md#the-slime).

## Slimeshire

### Elder Plop (Built)

- **Who:** a patient elder who watches over the village path. "Keep your goo
  bright and your eyes open. The forest has grown restless lately."
- **Role:** the mentor and Chapter 1's main quest giver. In Chapter 2 Plop asks
  to see real iron gear. In the game he greets the slime in the clearing at
  the start (Built).
- **Quests** (as built). Quests no longer make sense (owner, 2026-10-15: NPCs
  give info and tips, perhaps with one main quest for the Pearls, question
  50). Planned in their place (owner, 2026-10-16, question 60): the journal
  keeps one main quest, "Win back the Pearls", and the tips NPCs give, and an
  NPC hands over or teaches its find when the slime talks to it. An NPC with
  a tip that matters to the story shows the exclamation mark (owner,
  2026-10-16, question 82).
  - A Place to Work. It already teaches the stone tool recipes; Planned: Plop
    also hands over the first bag here. The stone tools go with the crafted
    tools (2026-10-15). Release 1 has one bag (question 53): Plop's 10-slot
    bag, and the 20-slot bag waits for the next release (owner, 2026-10-16,
    question 74).
  - Stone Tools, which teaches the Roll today (the Dodge until 2026-10-09).
    In the plan young Burr teaches it, in Chapter 1 (owner, 2026-10-16,
    question 61).
  - The One-Eyed Guardian. Its Squash Slam reward goes: the Slam is removed
    (2026-10-15).
  - Iron Gear.
  - Proposal: A Home of Your Own, the house quest. The vault waits for a
    later release (owner, 2026-10-16, question 53). *Closed 2026-10-16
    (question 11): Release 1 has no quests, so there is no house quest; an
    NPC hands the home over.*
- **Planned tip (owner, 2026-10-16, question 61):** after the Spear, Plop says
  the worms have shut their burrow with the old Thorn Guard gate, and only
  **Burr**, the gatekeeper in the West village, knew its trick.
- **Planned first meeting (owner, 2026-10-16, question 100):** Plop meets the
  slime at the town's edge as it comes out of the forest after the prologue,
  amazed: "No slime has come out of the Wellspring in years... and here you
  are." He gives it the 10-slot bag and its first tips, with the exclamation
  mark, and later sends it to Burr (the tip above). He no longer greets it at
  the Wellspring.
- **Proposal:**
  - The only one old enough to remember Gorge and the shattering. Plop tells the
    story a piece at a time, one piece per Pearl returned.
  - Plop is the first to see the slime born and calls it a sign. *Changed
    2026-10-16: the slime is born alone in the prologue and meets Yoi first;
    Plop is the first it meets in town.*

### Mossy (Built)

- **Who:** a purple scout, always a step ahead. "Psst! Over here. Keep your
  voice down."
- **Role:** the main quest giver at the front line. In Chapter 1 Mossy gives
  Worm Trouble, which prepares the slime to face Fatty One Eye. In Chapter 2
  Mossy moves to the forest camp and gives Beyond the Verdant Gate, Tom in the
  Web (the rescue of Tom), A Harder Pick, and The Matron's Nest (as built;
  quests no longer make sense, and NPCs give tips in their place, question
  60). In the build Beyond the Verdant Gate teaches Stretch Lash, which waits
  for the next release in the plan (owner, 2026-10-16, question 94).
  Fatty's Pearl grants the Heavy skin, and the Matron's Pearl the Silk skin.
- **Proposal:** the recurring scout. Mossy arrives in every new region first,
  sets up a camp, and is waiting there when the slime arrives. This is a little
  like the owl in Zelda. Mossy tracks the Pearls' trail toward Volcano Ridge.

### Tom (Built, 2026-10-09)

- **Who:** a slime who already knows how to swallow. He went into a spider
  thicket in Gloop Forest to swallow some silk and got caught in a web.
- **Look:** a round blue jelly slime with a moss-green scarf and a small
  bottle of glowing essence. The art prompt asked for amber-orange; the model
  made him blue, and the blue was kept because it reads well and differs from
  the other NPCs.
- **Role:** Tom in the Web. Mossy asks the slime to free him; the slime hits
  the web around him with its weapon. Back at Mossy's forest camp, where he
  waits by the fire, Tom teaches **Swallow**
  ([Chapter 2](chapters/chapter-2-gloop-forest.md), Beat 3), with the full
  ceremony (built 2026-10-09).
- **2026-10-15:** Swallow is marked for deletion, so Tom has nothing left to
  teach. **Open:** he teaches Chapter 2's new key tool instead, or his beat
  goes (question 31). He hangs in a cocoon, which a weapon can tear before
  the slime has the Silk skin (owner, 2026-10-16, question 54).
  *2026-10-16 (owner, question 94): Chapter 2's key tool is the Goo Bomb;
  whether Tom teaches it is question 95. The weapon that tears his cocoon is
  the Spear, Release 1's only shape.* *Changed 2026-10-16 (sixth round,
  owner, question 95): Tom teaches the **Goo Bomb**, with the full ceremony.
  Reaching him is not easy: the slime passes a maze with several secrets and
  tests, solved with the powers it has by then (the Spear, the Roll, the Heavy
  skin and the Gulp). Its webs slow the slime and cannot be cut before the
  Silk skin.* *Changed 2026-10-16 (seventh round, owner, question 113): Tom
  hangs in his cocoon at the end of the maze and teaches the Goo Bomb right
  there, as soon as he is freed.* *Since 2026-10-16 (eighth round, questions
  129 and 132): his cocoon hangs by a thread with his face showing, unlike a
  silk-wrapped prize or the Cocoon Mimic, and a cracked wall by him, his
  maze's way back, is the Goo Bomb's first lock.*
- **Open:** his voice and the rest of his story; the owner may add more later.

### Pip (Built)

- **Who:** the smith's son, who has wanted to light the Forge again since the
  worms came. "My dad was Slimeshire's smith, before the worms chased him off."
- **Role:** Rekindle the Forge (Chapter 2). Planned: the Forge brews the
  potions until evolutions arrive (owner, 2026-10-15, question 51).
- **Planned (owner, 2026-10-16, question 59):** his story stays relighting
  the Forge. It is no longer about finding his dad: the smith is safe at his
  crystal mine and comes home once the Crystal Colossus falls.
- **Planned (owner, 2026-10-16, question 79):** Pearl 2, the Matron's,
  relights the Forge when it comes home, and Pip and the slime light it. This
  replaces Rekindle the Forge and its ore cost. From then on the Forge brews
  the potions and knows their recipes (question 82). *Changed 2026-10-16
  (seventh round, question 110): Pearl 2 goes home to the Wellspring, and its
  power flows into the town and lights the Forge.*
- **Proposal:** Pip grows from a kid with a dream into Slimeshire's new
  smith.

### Lili (Built)

- **Who:** the friendliest slime in the clearing. "There is always room for one
  more friend in our clearing."
- **Role:** A Tonic for Lili, the optional quest that teaches the healing
  tonic. Planned: she tells of the hideouts slimes dug during Gorge's war,
  which leads the slime to Yoi's Hideout in her west yard (owner, 2026-10-11;
  question 50). *Changed 2026-10-16 (question 99): her tip now leads to Yoi's
  home, an optional secret.* *Since the eighth round (question 128) the heap
  over its hatch is crates, not bricks.*
- **Proposal:** the town's heart. Lili notices every change in Slimeshire as
  Pearls return and welcomes each slime who comes back.

### Lily the Fishergirl (Built)

- **Who:** a patient fisher at the lake. "The big one always bites right after

- **Role:** Snack for the Road (optional).
- **Planned:** renamed **Dora** (owner, 2026-10-11; [question 7](07-decisions-archive.md#story)),

### Old Veteran Yoi (Planned)

- **Who:** an old soldier of the Thorn Guard from Gorge's war. He held the
  gate against Gorge's monsters; when the war was lost and Slimeshire hid
  under its own floor, he stayed out to keep watch. *Since 2026-10-16
  (seventh round, question 108): pages of the Thorn Guard's war journal,
  Release 1's lore manuscripts, tell the war bit by bit.*
- **Look** (the 2026-10-11 plan's): faded moss-grey jelly, an eye patch, a
  chipped bone-white spike where young slimes have a sprout, and a red war
  sash with a bronze thorn badge.
- **Role (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16,
  [questions 97 and 99](07-decisions-archive.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15)):**
  - In the [prologue](../design/2026-10-16-prologue.md) he fights four of
    Fatty One Eye's worms in his spear form, drops two with one thrust and
    falls wounded: "You there! Kid! Throw something, anything!" The slime
    saves him by throwing things.
  - He tells his story in a few lines and teaches the **Spear** for the
    slime's help, with the full ceremony. Draft line: "Gather all of
    yourself, and push it out to one point."
  - He limps off: "Find old Yoi if you need a soldier's advice." His home,
    under Lili's west yard, is an optional secret: inside are a heart
    quarter, his tips, and later the Spear's upgrades. *Changed 2026-10-16
    (seventh round, question 111): who upgrades the Spear, Yoi's techniques
    or the Forge, is decided after Release 1.*
- **The skeletons** (owner, 2026-10-16, question 125): the dungeons'
  skeletons are his fallen comrades of the Thorn Guard, whose bones Gorge's
  vassals raised to guard the old forts, and Yoi knows their old helmets.
- *Until 2026-10-16: he taught the Spear in his den at the end of Yoi's
  Hideout, a war hideout under Lili's west yard (owner, 2026-10-11,
  confirmed on 2026-10-15 in question 50; the
  [dungeons plan](../design/2026-10-11-release-1-dungeons-plan.md#4-yois-hideout)).*
- Not built: no art, scene or dialogue yet.

### The worm who surrenders (Idea)

From the roadmap's parking lot: a named worm who gives up at low HP. If the
slime spares him, he becomes Slimeshire's shopkeeper, which also gives coins a

## The West village

Part of the West village joins Release 1 (owner, 2026-10-16;
[World](03-world.md#the-west-village)): a small town with Burr's house, three
or four more houses, a shop and a game to play (question 73). The shop sells
potions and food ingredients (question 88), and a secret there teaches a
simpler potion (question 89). Who else lives there, and who runs the shop,
is not decided yet. The gatekeeper's family lives there:
Burr and young Burr, a family of its own, while the smith is Pip's father
(the "two families" fix, chosen 2026-10-16).

### Burr, the gatekeeper (Planned)

- **Who:** the gatekeeper of the old Thorn Guard gate, the only one who knew
  its trick.
- **Role (owner, 2026-10-16,
  [question 61](07-decisions-archive.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15)):**
  - He went to fight Fatty One Eye and never came back. The worms then shut
    their burrow with his old gate.
  - In the first half of Fatty's Lair he sits in a cell. A small key found in
    the lair frees him; he thanks the slime and asks it to visit him at home.
    *Since 2026-10-16 (eighth round, question 134): his cell lies on the
    lair's main path, so freeing him cannot be skipped.*
  - Back home, he gives his thanks: an energy upgrade or a heart (which one
    is not decided; max energy grows only through secrets and bosses,
    question 19). *Changed 2026-10-16 (owner, question 80): his thanks is the
    **Bow**. It was to come after Release 1, so when it comes is
    question 85.* *Changed again 2026-10-16 (owner, questions 85 and 94):
    the Bow is in Release 1, in Chapter 3. Burr gives it when the slime
    comes back to the West village after Chapter 2, no longer right after
    Fatty's Lair.* *Changed 2026-10-16 (sixth round, questions 102 and
    106): visited before Pearl 2, he thanks the slime and gives a tip: "I am
    making you something, come back when the forest is free." He needs silk
    and other things to build it. The silk is the **Matron's thread**, a
    spool the slime finds in her nest after she falls (with the Silk skin it
    can take it from her web); the other things are already in his workshop.
    With the thread and Pearl 2 home, he teaches the **Bow**: one find, one
    trip back, and no crafting.* *Since 2026-10-16 (eighth round, questions
    132 and 134): the Bow's first targets stand in his yard, and the smith at
    the mine camp sends the slime to him.* *Burr's thanks once weighed "max
    energy grows only through secrets and bosses"; since the eighth round
    potions, the well and bosses raise it (question 19).*
  - **After Fatty** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 103): freed, he
    says the town must see the Pearl, and the journal's main quest points
    home.
- Not built: no art, scene or dialogue yet.
