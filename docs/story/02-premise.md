# Premise

> **Everything on this page is a Proposal** unless it is tagged Built. It grows
> out of what the game already says:
> - Elder Plop welcomes "a new slime in the clearing".
> - The worms chased the smith away and the Forge went cold.
> - "The forest has grown restless lately."
> - Fatty One Eye has always guarded the key to "Gloop Forest, and the rest of
>   the world".

## Logline

A newborn slime bubbles up from the last living drop of Slimeshire's
Wellspring and sets out to win back the Wellspring's Pearls before all is lost
to Gorge. Gorge, the greedy slime who shattered the Wellspring, gave the Pearls
to his allies, the bosses. He plans to rule the world and enslave the slimes
who are left. Each Pearl holds a power. Used wisely, together they can create
new slimes; used without wisdom, they create monsters. Gorge uses them to make
his aberrations.

## Backstory

- **The Wellspring.** Slimeshire grew around a pool of living goo called the
  Wellspring, and every slime was born from it. While its heart shone, the land
  was peaceful and Slimeshire thrived.
- **Gorge's betrayal.** Long ago, within Elder Plop's lifetime, Gorge mastered
  the Gulp and became obsessed with its power. Feeling unstoppable, he tried to
  devour the Wellspring's heart, but its power was too great even for him.
- **The eight Pearls.** Unable to consume the heart, Gorge shattered it into
  eight Pearls. He gave one to each of his eight most loyal vassals, granting
  them extraordinary powers. Fatty One Eye is one of these eight bosses.
- **The eight regions.** Each boss seized control of one region and has
  terrorized its inhabitants ever since. Their rule displaced creatures, drove
  the smith away, left the Forge cold and reduced the Workshop to ruins. The
  smith now works his crystal mine in the caverns, which the Crystal Colossus
  has blocked (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16).
- **The war** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16,
  [prologue](../design/2026-10-16-prologue.md)). Old Veteran Yoi of the
  Thorn Guard held the gate against Gorge's monsters in the great war. When
  the war was lost, Slimeshire hid under its own floor; Yoi stayed out to keep
  watch.
- **The dimming.** Without its heart, the Wellspring began to fade, and no new
  slime had been born in years.
- **Now.** While his eight vassals hold the kingdom, Gorge prepares his final
  strike from a castle deep inside a volcano. To save the kingdom and restore
  the Wellspring, the hero must defeat all eight bosses, recover the Pearls and
  finally confront Gorge, the ninth and final boss.

## The slime

- **Born in the first minute of the game** from the Wellspring's last drop: the
  first new slime in years. That is why it starts with nothing, not even a bag,
  knows nothing, and learns to survive and fight from the townsfolk. It fits
  the line already in the game: Plop says "Welcome, little one".
  *Changed 2026-10-16 (Planned, question 97): the prologue shows the birth.
  The slime learns to move and to gulp on its own, and its first teacher is
  Yoi, the veteran it saves, who teaches it the Spear.*
- **A silent hero**, as in Zelda. Others talk to it, and it answers with
  actions and squishy reactions.
- **Name: Open.** The game is named after Isa, one of its creators. Whether the
  hero is called Isa is the owner's call.
- **The Pearls give it its skins.** Each Pearl holds one skin. When the slime
  frees a Pearl from a boss, the skin passes permanently into the slime during
  the item ceremony, and it stays when the Pearl returns to the Wellspring.
  Fatty One Eye's Pearl grants the **Heavy** skin and the Matron's the
  **Silk** skin (Planned, owner, 2026-10-15), and the Crystal Colossus's the
  **Feral** skin (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16). The other five are Open; the
  **Swim** skin goes to one of those later Pearls, after Release 1 (owner,
  2026-10-16). Shapes and
  abilities come from the townsfolk and from old manuscripts; in Release 1
  the townsfolk teach and chests hold the finds, and manuscript pieces come
  later (see
  [Direction](01-direction.md#the-slimes-powers)). Release 1 gives eight
  powers besides the Gulp, about three a chapter, and the War Axe and Stretch
  Lash wait for the next release (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 94).
- **The Gulp is its gift**, the same power Gorge abused. The hero only grabs
  and throws with it, and it gives back what it wins: every Pearl goes home to
  the Wellspring. Gorge gulped to *own*.

## Goal and shape of the story

1. **Each region holds one Pearl** inside its boss. Beating the boss frees the
   Pearl and permanently grants its skin in the full item ceremony.
2. **Returning a Pearl to the Wellspring** makes Slimeshire visibly come back
   to life. A building is restored, slimes who fled return, and the Wellspring
   shines a little brighter. This uses the town growth the guidelines already
   promise ("Slimeshire visibly grows as the story advances"). A building
   restored by a returned Pearl is Planned (owner, 2026-10-16, question 60):
   Pearl 2, the Matron's, relights the Forge, and Pip and the slime light it
   (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 79). Pearl 1, Fatty One Eye's,
   restores Slimeshire's well, which then refills the slime's energy
   (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 90). *Changed 2026-10-16 (sixth
   round): each Pearl's homecoming is a short scene at the Wellspring
   (question 98). After Fatty, the freed Burr says the town must see the
   Pearl, the journal points home, and the well comes back when the slime
   brings the Pearl to it (question 103); it gives a full refill, a drink in
   town (question 105).*
3. **Midpoint** (Open: which chapter): Gorge wakes. The rumbling becomes
   eruptions, Gorge steals the returned Pearls back and holds them all again,
   and the stakes rise. The slime keeps its skins.
4. **Finale: Volcano Ridge.** Gorge has become a giant molten slime who has
   gulped everything around him. He fights with every Pearl, one at a time,
   and the slime answers with every skin it has earned.
5. **Ending.** The hero could gulp the last Pearl and become the strongest
   slime alive, and instead returns it. The Wellspring shines again and new
   slimes are born. Emptied of everything he swallowed, Gorge shrinks back
   into a small, ordinary slime. He is not destroyed; maybe he even moves into
   Slimeshire.

## Tone

- **Cozy storybook**, like the art style: warm and funny, with real danger but
  no gore.
- **Monsters are not evil.** Most are displaced, or swollen by a Pearl.
  Bosses are beaten, not killed: when the Pearl leaves them, they shrink back
  and calm down.
- **Themes:**
  - growing up (a newborn learns everything);
  - community (rebuilding Slimeshire together);
  - sharing instead of hoarding (the Gulp).

## What the premise asks of the game

These are new pieces the story would need. None are built yet.

- **The Wellspring:** the prologue's lake, a pool of glowing goo ringed by
  forest in the south, between chunks B4 and C4, where the slime is born.
  After the prologue a short forest path from Slimeshire's south-west reaches
  it, and each Pearl's homecoming is a short scene there (Planned, owner,
  2026-10-16, question 98). It brightens with each Pearl, and the town
  changes as the Pearls come home. It also refills the slime's energy in full
  (Planned, owner, 2026-10-15), and a campfire stands by it (question 104).
  *Until 2026-10-16 it was its own new map east of level-1, the game's first
  map.*
- **The prologue** (Planned, owner, 2026-10-16, question 97;
  [prologue design](../design/2026-10-16-prologue.md)): the slime is born at
  the Wellspring, learns to move and to gulp, sees Yoi fall to four worms,
  saves him by throwing things, and learns the Spear from him. A barred gate
  opens into Slimeshire, and at the town's edge Plop meets it, amazed, and
  gives it the 10-slot bag and its first tips (question 100). *Until
  2026-10-16 the opening scene had Plop arrive at the Wellspring, teach the
  slime to move and ask it to meet him in town.*
- **Pearls:** one story item and one skin per regional boss. The ceremony
  grants the skin permanently before the Pearl returns to the Wellspring.
- **Beaten bosses** do not come back (today a boss respawns every five
  minutes).
