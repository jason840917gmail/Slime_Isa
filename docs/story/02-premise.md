## Backstory

- **The Wellspring.** Slimeshire grew around a pool of living goo called the
  Wellspring, and every slime was born from it. While its heart shone, the land
  was peaceful and Slimeshire thrived.
- **Gorge's betrayal.** Long ago, within Elder Plop's lifetime, Gorge mastered
  the Gulp and became obsessed with its power. Feeling unstoppable, he tried to
  devour the Wellspring's heart, but its power was too great even for him.
- **The eight Pearls.** Unable to consume the heart, Gorge shattered it into
  eight Pearls. He gave one to each of his eight most loyal vassals, granting
  them extraordinary powers. Fatty One Eye is one of these eight bosses.
- **The eight regions.** Each boss seized control of one region and has
  terrorized its inhabitants ever since. Their rule displaced creatures, drove
  the smith away, left the Forge cold and reduced the Workshop to ruins.
- **The dimming.** Without its heart, the Wellspring began to fade, and no new
  slime had been born in years.
- **Now.** While his eight vassals hold the kingdom, Gorge prepares his final
  strike from a castle deep inside a volcano. To save the kingdom and restore
  the Wellspring, the hero must defeat all eight bosses, recover the Pearls and
  finally confront Gorge, the ninth and final boss.

## The slime

- **Born in the first minute of the game** from the Wellspring's last drop: the
  first new slime in years. That is why it starts with nothing, not even a bag,
  knows nothing, and learns to survive and fight from the townsfolk. It fits
  the line already in the game: Plop says "Welcome, little one".
- **A silent hero**, as in Zelda. Others talk to it, and it answers with
  actions and squishy reactions.
- **Name: Open.** The game is named after Isa, one of its creators. Whether the
  hero is called Isa is the owner's call.
- **The Pearls grant its powers.** Each Pearl holds one unique ability. When
  the slime frees a Pearl from a boss, that ability passes permanently into
  the slime during the item ceremony. The Pearl can then return to the
  Wellspring without taking the ability back. Fatty One Eye's Pearl grants
  Jump, and the Matron's Pearl grants Stretch Lash. The other six abilities
  are Open.
- **The Gulp is its gift**, the same power that ruined Gorge. That gives the
  story its question: can a slime gulp without becoming greedy? The hero gulps
  to *learn* (each armor is gulped once and kept). Gorge gulped to *own*.

## Goal and shape of the story

1. **Each region holds one Pearl** inside its boss. Beating the boss frees the
  Pearl and permanently grants its unique ability in the full item ceremony.
2. **Returning a Pearl to the Wellspring** makes Slimeshire visibly come back
   to life. A building is restored, slimes who fled return, and the Wellspring
   shines a little brighter. This uses the town growth the guidelines already
   promise ("Slimeshire visibly grows as the story advances").
3. **Midpoint** (Open: which chapter): Gorge wakes. The rumbling becomes
   eruptions, a Pearls are stolen back, and now Gorge have all the pearls, and
   the stakes rise. 
4. **Finale: Volcano Ridge.** Gorge has become a giant molten slime who has
   gulped everything around him. The last fight uses every armor the slime has
   earned. in the final battle Gorge is able to use all the pearls but one at the time to fight you.
5. **Ending.** The hero could gulp the last Pearl and become the strongest
   slime alive, and instead returns it. The Wellspring shines again and new
   slimes are born. Emptied of everything he swallowed, Gorge shrinks back
   into a small, ordinary slime. He is not destroyed; maybe he even moves into
   Slimeshire.

## Tone

- **Cozy storybook**, like the art style: warm and funny, with real danger but
  no gore.
- **Monsters are not evil.** Most are displaced, or swollen by a Pearl.
  Bosses are beaten, not killed: when the Pearl leaves them, they shrink back
  and calm down.
- **Themes:**
  - growing up (a newborn learns everything);
  - community (rebuilding Slimeshire together);
  - sharing instead of hoarding (the Gulp).
