# Premise

> **Everything on this page is a Proposal** unless it is tagged Built. It grows
> out of what the game already says:
> - Elder Plop welcomes "a new slime in the clearing".
> - The worms chased the smith away and the Forge went cold.
> - "The forest has grown restless lately."
> - Fatty One Eye has always guarded the key to "Gloop Forest, and the rest of
>   the world".

## Logline

A newborn slime bubbles up from the last living drop of Slimeshire's
Wellspring and sets out to bring back the Wellspring's scattered Pearls before
Gorge wakes. Gorge is the greedy slime who shattered the Wellspring, and once
awake he will swallow the world.

## Backstory

- **The Wellspring.** Slimeshire grew around a pool of living goo, the
  Wellspring, and every slime was born from it. While its heart shone, the land
  was calm and Slimeshire was full of slimes.
- **Gorge.** Long ago, within Elder Plop's lifetime, a slime named Gorge
  learned the Gulp: eat something and take its power. He could not stop. He
  ate rocks, then trees, then whole hills. In the end he tried to gulp the
  Wellspring's heart itself. The heart shattered into **Pearls** that flew
  across the land. Gorge, swollen and burning from the inside, rolled away into
  Volcano Ridge and hardened in its rock.
- **The Pearls changed what they touched.** A blob that swallowed one grew huge
  and one-eyed: Fatty One Eye. A spider that nested on one became the mother of
  a whole forest of weavers: the Matron. One sank into the caverns, and crystal
  has been growing over everything there ever since.
- **Everything shifted.** Creatures pushed out of their homes pushed into other
  creatures' homes. The worms came to Slimeshire, the smith fled, the Forge went
  cold, and the Workshop fell to ruin. These are Built facts in today's
  dialogue; the Pearls are the proposed reason behind them.
- **The dimming.** Without its heart the Wellspring dims, and no new slime has
  been born in years.
- **Now.** The ground has started to rumble. Gorge is stirring, and the Pearls
  are slowly being pulled toward Volcano Ridge. That is why "the forest has
  grown restless".

## The slime

- **Born in the first minute of the game** from the Wellspring's last drop: the
  first new slime in years. That is why it starts with nothing, not even a bag,
  knows nothing, and learns to dodge, jump and fight from the townsfolk. It fits
  the lines already in the game: Plop says "Welcome, little one", and Mossy
  says "you move like a puddle... squish down, then spring!"
- **A silent hero**, as in Zelda. Others talk to it, and it answers with
  actions and squishy reactions.
- **Name: Open.** The game is named after Isa, one of its creators. Whether the
  hero is called Isa is the owner's call.
- **The Gulp is its gift**, the same power that ruined Gorge. That gives the
  story its question: can a slime gulp without becoming greedy? The hero gulps
  to *learn* (each armor is gulped once and kept). Gorge gulped to *own*.

## Goal and shape of the story

1. **Each region holds one Pearl**, usually inside its boss. Beating the boss
   frees the Pearl, which gets the full item ceremony.
2. **Returning a Pearl to the Wellspring** makes Slimeshire visibly come back
   to life. A building is restored, slimes who fled return, and the Wellspring
   shines a little brighter. This uses the town growth the guidelines already
   promise ("Slimeshire visibly grows as the story advances").
3. **Midpoint** (Open: which chapter): Gorge wakes. The rumbling becomes
   eruptions, a Pearl is stolen back, or an attack hits Slimeshire itself, and
   the stakes rise.
4. **Finale: Volcano Ridge.** Gorge has become a giant molten slime who has
   gulped everything around him. The last fight uses every armor the slime has
   earned.
5. **Ending.** The hero could gulp the last Pearl and become the strongest
   slime alive, and instead returns it. The Wellspring shines again and new
   slimes are born. Emptied of everything he swallowed, Gorge shrinks back
   into a small, ordinary slime. He is not destroyed; maybe he even moves into
   Slimeshire.

## Tone

- **Cozy storybook**, like the art style: warm and funny, with real danger but
  no gore.
- **Monsters are not evil.** Most are displaced, or swollen by a Pearl.
  Bosses are beaten, not killed: when the Pearl leaves them, they shrink back
  and calm down.
- **Themes:**
  - growing up (a newborn learns everything);
  - community (rebuilding Slimeshire together);
  - sharing instead of hoarding (the Gulp).

## What the premise asks of the game

These are new pieces the story would need. None are built yet.

- **The Wellspring:** a pool in Slimeshire, either in the plaza or in the
  clearing where the slime is born (Open). It brightens per Pearl.
- **An opening scene:** the slime is born from the Wellspring and Plop
  arrives.
- **Pearls:** one story item per boss, with the ceremony and a "return it to
  the Wellspring" moment.
- **Boss respawn:** once a boss's Pearl is gone, should the boss still respawn
  every five minutes as today? (Open.)
