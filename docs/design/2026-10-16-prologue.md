## The idea

Zelda: A Link to the Past opens on a rainy night that ends with the sword; Super Metroid opens on
Ceres Station. Both are short, closed places that teach the controls, tell the first piece of the
story, and give one power before the big world opens. Slime Isa gets the same: a few minutes from the
slime's birth to its first shape, the Spear.

The owner's outline:
1. The slime wakes at the slime lake where it is born, and a small tutorial teaches it to move.
2. It learns to gulp, to clear objects that block its way.
3. The camera moves to show the veteran slime fighting four worms in his spear form. He kills two,
   falls wounded and calls for help.
4. The slime saves him with the only weapon it has: it sucks up objects and throws them at the
   worms, which die from a single hit.
5. The veteran tells his story (he fought in the great war) and teaches the slime the Spear for its
   help.

**Where:** a lake ringed by forest in the south, between chunks B4 and C4. The place fills the whole
screen, so neither the West village nor Slimeshire can be seen from it, and there is not much else to
do there. The slime is born in B4; walking east into C4, it sees the attack. *Changed 2026-10-16
(question 112): the Wellspring takes B4's south edge, sealed off by forest, so the West village's
town fits in B4's north and A4.*

## The prologue, polished

### Screen 1: the Wellspring (B4)

- **Dawn.** A round pool of glowing green goo in a clearing ringed by tall trees, mist on the water,
  fireflies going home. The title fades. No HUD yet.
- **The birth.** A bubble rises, wobbles and pops: the last slime born from the Wellspring opens its
  eyes. There is no voice and no text, only the scene. It is the premise's "last drop". *Changed
  2026-10-16 (question 113): the HUD appears right after this scene.*
- **Moving.** The first-time control hints (built, roadmap 4.6) show the arrows. A few things to walk
  to: a firefly that drifts away, the pool's edge with its wading splash.
- **The way is blocked.** The only path out of the clearing is choked with light things: clay pots, a
  crate and a mossy log end. The hint shows Q: hold to suck, tap to throw. The slime clears the path
  one piece at a time, and the last pot breaks against a rock with a crash. The jingle plays as the
  path opens: the first secret is the way out.

### Screen 2: the glade (C4's south-west edge)

- **The attack (a short scene).** As the slime steps into the glade, the camera takes over and pans
  ahead.
  - An old slime with an eye patch and a red war sash stands against four worms. A spear grows out of
    his own body.
  - He thrusts once, and two worms on one line fall: this shows the Spear before the slime has it.
  - A third bites him from the side. He drops to one knee, his spear melting back into him: "You
    there! Kid! Throw something, anything!"
  - The camera comes back to the slime.
- **The rescue.** The two worms left turn on the slime. The glade is scattered with pots, stones and
  a crate: light things to suck and throw.
  - Each worm dies from one throw (weak prologue worms with a single hit point).
  - The worms can hurt the slime, so it learns to move while aiming. If it faints, it wakes at the
    glade's edge with the worms back where they were; the prologue has no game over.
- **The veteran.** He is **Yoi of the Thorn Guard** (his look from the 2026-10-11 plan: faded
  moss-grey jelly, an eye patch, a chipped bone-white spike where young slimes have a sprout, a red
  war sash with a bronze thorn badge).
  - He tells his story in a few lines. He held the gate against Gorge's monsters in the great war.
    When the war was lost, Slimeshire hid under its own floor. He stayed out to keep watch, and the
    worms are Fatty One Eye's, grown bold.
  - He teaches the Spear for the slime's help: "Gather all of yourself, and push it out to one
    point." The full "New shape" ceremony plays.
- **The first lock.** The glade's way out to Slimeshire is an old Thorn Guard gate, shut and worked
  from the far side. Its lever is behind the bars, and only a thrust fits between them: the Spear's
  own lock ("through bars"), taught at once. *Since 2026-10-16 (question 114): Spear-only bells, on
  an islet or behind a gap, which a thrown pot cannot ring, are its second kind of lock across the
  world.* *Changed in the eighth round (questions 115 and 131): no bell is the Spear's, and bars
  stop arrows, so a lever behind bars stays the Spear's alone.*
- **Goodbye.** Yoi limps off: "Find old Yoi if you need a soldier's advice. Now go, the town needs
  every slime it has." The slime walks out of the forest, and the screen opens wide on Slimeshire.

### Then

- At the town's edge Elder Plop meets the slime, amazed: "No slime has come out of the Wellspring in
  years... and here you are." The main game begins (Chapter 1).
- Play time: about 3 to 5 minutes.

## What it teaches

| Step | The player learns |
|---|---|
| The birth | The world's look; the premise without words |
| Moving | The arrows; wading |
| The blocked path | The Gulp: suck, throw; that things block the way and can be cleared |
| The attack | The Spear exists, and what it does (two foes on one line) |
| The rescue | Fighting by throwing; moving under attack |
| The veteran | The war, Gorge, the worms; the Spear and its ceremony |
| The gate | The Spear's own kind of lock |

## What changes elsewhere

- **The Spear** is learned in the prologue, not in Yoi's Hideout. The hideout becomes Yoi's home:
  Lili's tip and the heap stay as an optional secret, with a heart quarter, Yoi's tips and later
  the Spear's upgrades (question 99). *Changed 2026-10-16 (question 111): who upgrades the Spear,
  Yoi's techniques or the Forge, is decided after Release 1.*
- **The Wellspring** is the prologue's lake (question 98). After the prologue a short forest path
  from Slimeshire's south-west keeps it in reach, and each Pearl's homecoming is a short scene there.
  *Changed 2026-10-16: the path leaves Slimeshire, not the West village's town (question 112).
  Every Pearl goes home to it, it brightens, and its power flows into the town: the well comes back
  with Pearl 1, the Forge lights with Pearl 2 (question 110).*
- **Elder Plop** meets the slime at the town's edge as it comes out of the forest, gives it the
  10-slot bag and its first tips, and later sends it to Burr (question 100).
- **Release 1** gains the prologue before Chapter 1.
