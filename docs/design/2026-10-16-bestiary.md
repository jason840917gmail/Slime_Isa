# Bestiary: Release 1's Enemies

*Status: a proposal for the owner (2026-10-16), who asked for at least 20 different enemies, with
unique ones in each dungeon, not all of them walking at the slime, some following a strict pattern,
some easy to beat with the right power, and ideas from other games. Built ones are marked. The
owner's pick is question 107 in
[Open questions](../story/07-decisions-archive.md#contradictions-left-after-the-review-2026-10-15).*

## The rules every enemy follows

- **A tell.** Each enemy shows what it is about to do: a pose, a sound, a shadow, a glow. Under the
  clarity rule ([Direction](../story/01-direction.md)), no two enemies look alike, and a dangerous one
  never looks like a harmless one.
- **Always beatable, easy with the right tool.** Every enemy can be beaten with the Spear and the
  Roll, except the shadows (the Gloomling), which nothing hurts: they are chased off by light, and
  they appear only where the slime already has the Lantern. Its right tool makes an enemy a one- or
  two-hit job. No enemy outside a dungeon's own lesson
  requires a power ("no ability requires another").
- **A kind of its own.** Each enemy brings one way of moving or fighting that the others don't: a
  chaser, a keeper (keeps its distance), a lunger, a burrower, a turret, an ambusher, a swarm, a
  charger, a patroller (a fixed track), a flyer, a wall crawler, a sentinel (a beam), a thief, a
  mimic, a shadow (only light moves it), an armoured one.
- **Each dungeon** has two or three enemies of its own and a mini-boss, and they teach the
  dungeon's tool before its boss needs it.
- **Gorge's army looks bad** (owner, 2026-10-16). Worms of every kind make up most of Gorge's
  army ([Premise](../story/02-premise.md)): every worm (1 to 5, 12) has a mean face and war gear and
  wears the army's mark (the red scarf and Gorge's emblem). No enemy looks cute or scared.
- **The Gulp never grabs a live enemy** (Direction). It catches what enemies throw: seeds, mud,
  stones. Throwing it back is how the slime turns their attacks on them.
- **Two kinds of ranged attack** (owner, 2026-10-16), both aimed through
  `game/enemy/four_way_aim.gd`. A shooter first lines up with the slime on one of its lines, then
  shoots along it, and a slime that steps off the line during the tell is missed.
  - **Four directions** (straight up, down, left or right, like Zelda's archers): the Worm Archer
    and the spiders, who run fast to line up, and the turrets: the Spitcap, and the Pipe Worm
    straight out of its pipe.
  - **Eight directions** (the diagonals too): the skeletons (the Bone Thrower) and the Pick Mole.
  - The eyes' beams are neither: their gaze turns smoothly and beams the moment it meets the
    slime, like Zelda's Beamos. Lunges, leaps and charges are not shots, and the bosses keep their
    own patterns (question 126).

Release 1's powers, for the "right tool" column: the Gulp, the Spear, the Roll (from Chapter 1), the
Heavy skin (after Fatty One Eye), the Goo Bomb and the Silk skin (Chapter 2), the Lantern, the Bow
and the Feral skin (Chapter 3).

## Every dungeon: the skeletons and the Watcher Eye (owner, 2026-10-16)

The owner asked for them after the 29 were accepted: a skeleton family found **only in the
dungeons** (all three, from Fatty's Lair on) and a floating eye like Zelda's laser thrower, always
where there are statues or stands to hide behind. None of them chases the slime.

- **Where the skeletons come from** (owner, 2026-10-16, question 125): they are the fallen Thorn
  Guard soldiers of the great war. Gorge's vassals raised their bones (with a Pearl's power used
  without wisdom: his aberrations) to guard the dungeons, which were the old forts. They stir in the
  deep rooms of every dungeon from Fatty's Lair on, and Yoi knows their old helmets.

- **The bone pile** (all three skeletons, kept by the owner as built, 2026-10-16, question 123; 3 s, and the owner can switch
  it off: [BESTIARY.md](../godot/BESTIARY.md#the-skeletons-the-bone-pile-rows-30-to-32)): beaten, a
  skeleton falls into a pile of bones and stands up whole a few seconds later. Any hit on the pile scatters it for good, or the
  Gulp takes the skull from the pile (a pile is not a live enemy) and the slime can throw it like a
  pot; without its skull the skeleton cannot stand up.

## Counts

- 29 enemies: all 29 built (1 to 29; rows 4 to 14 and 18 to 29 on 2026-10-16), 5 planned in the
  2026-10-11 combat plan (11, 14, 18, 20, 22), and 18 new.
- The seventh row, every dungeon's (30 to 33): all four built (2026-10-16): the three skeletons and
  the Watcher Eye.
- Each dungeon has two or three of its own: the Pipe Worm and the Dung Roller (Fatty's Lair), the
  Broodling and the Cocoon Mimic (the Weaver Hollow), and the Caverns' Gloomling, Prism Eye and Pick
  Mole, with a mini-boss each.
- About half do not walk at the slime: turrets, patrollers, an ambusher, a burrower, flyers, a wall
  crawler, a sentinel and a thief.
- The bosses stay as planned: Fatty One Eye, the Orb-Weaver Matron and the Crystal Colossus.
  *Since 2026-10-16 (owner, question 133): the Matron lives at the top of the Weaver Hollow, and
  her fight needs the Goo Bomb: she shields herself in a web cocoon that only a blast tears open,
  then the Spear. Each boss also leaves a full heart (question 17).*
