# Game Guidelines

> **Status: incomplete living draft — resume later.**
>
> This document records agreed direction for the first version. It is not a
> complete production specification; unresolved topics remain open below.

## Core Loop

Explore and gather resources. Fight enemies for drops and access to dangerous
areas. Upgrade the home and its workstations. Craft stronger tools and
weapons, then tackle tougher enemies to obtain rarer materials.

## Home

The first version has one movable, upgradable home. It is functional, but not
automatically safe: map conditions and nearby enemies determine whether it is
safe to use.

## Crafting And Workstations

Simple survival recipes may be portable. Weapons, advanced tools, building
pieces, and other progression recipes require the correct home workstation.

Each workstation owns a clear recipe family. Upgrading a workstation unlocks
the next recipe tier in that family. Stations unlock gradually:

- **Kitchen:** food, healing, and buffs.
- **Workbench:** basic tools, repairs, storage, and building parts.
- **Forge:** weapons, metal tools, armor, and ore upgrades.
- **Alchemy table:** potions, antidotes, bombs, and monster-material recipes.
- **Builder’s table:** home upgrades, furniture, defenses, and moving the home.

Future possibilities such as a loom or enchanting station are examples only and
are not committed direction.

## Resource Progression

Tool-gated and enemy-gated progression work together. The player starts with
wood and stone, crafts basic harvesting tools, and uses improved tools to
harvest iron and later rarer materials in tougher regions. Enemies provide
special ingredients for high-tier weapons, potions, and upgrades.
