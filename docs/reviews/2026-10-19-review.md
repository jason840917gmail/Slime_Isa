# Review: the work of 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-19 (2a04028b, 442dd8b2, 0a6b9954)

Reviewed: the commits after `48a7b514`: the 2026-10-17 snapshot, the unified fences, C4's props and
first bell, D5's moat, Fatty's Lair as one world per room, the bevel interior walls, levels in rooms
and painted level collision, the dividers, the music, and the smaller pieces. I read the code against
its docs, decoded the Lair's room scenes (doors, records, paint) with scratch scripts, ran
`tools/verify_scenes.gd` and the full suite once, and fixed the high findings that were clearly bugs
with a safe, general change. Everything else is listed for the owner to choose. Nothing is committed.

**Someone else edited files while this review ran.** These changes are not mine and were not
reviewed: `player.gd` (`arrive_at`), `dungeon.gd`, `stair_hatch.gd`, `test_fattys_lair.gd`,
`ELEVATION.md`, `r7-bell-bridge.tscn` and its elevation cache, and `fence_tileset.tres`.

## For the owner, in short

**What works.** These all read correct:

- **Room travel.** The 54 room doors pair up both ways (27 pairs, opposite walls), and the slide and
  the iris go to the right doors. Each `requires` names a record that exists.
- **Records.** They keep the ids of the old single world, so saves from before the switch still fit.
- **The seal and resuming.** The seal lets the slime go from room to room and refuses the way out. A
  quit resumes at the entrance.
- **Shared code with outdoors.** Ledge drops, slabs and hazards use the outdoor code in rooms. No
  branch is keyed on a room id or node name.
- **Smaller pieces.** The heart in a chest is saved once. The planks' times use the gameplay clock.
  The watch-post records (bell, gate, chest) are the same as before.

**Broken and fixed here (4).**

1. **The interior-wall layer deleted door objects.** In the editor it deleted doors along with
   their Inspector settings, and undo could not bring them back. Example: erase one cell of the
   hub's boss door block, then Ctrl+Z. The door returns as `r3-pit-hall-boss-26-0` with a new
   `door_id`, so saves lose its record.
2. **The fence layer deleted divider gates.** It did the same to the gates you had set up. Gates it
   made by painting also all shared one record (`divider-gate`).
3. **Fences did not collide in rooms without painted levels.** The Weigh House, the eye hall and
   the lower hall have none, so the slime, enemies and statues walked through every fence there.
   The Weigh House pens could be skipped.
4. **Every room change showed a banner made from the room's id.** For example "Fatty Lair R2 Slab
   Steps". Fatty's Lair now shows one banner, on the way in.

**Needs your decision (high, not fixed).** These need art, scene edits or a design choice:

- **Room corners collide on rounded outdoor shapes.** The faces are drawn square, so at every corner
  the collision and the drawing disagree.
- **A painted bridge never holds the slime at its sides.** Walking off a slab's side means falling
  at once.
- **A shut divider gate leaves a 31-unit hole on each side, which the slime fits through.** This
  affects the Old Armoury's chest and R7's key. The art shows the same gaps.
- **Some pockets can be walked around in the parent room's world.** In the hub, the slime can walk
  past Burr's cell door into the dark cell area. In the lower hall it can walk past the alcove's
  gate. The room edges of Burr's cell and the R5 pockets are fieldstone walls and fence dividers,
  not bevel faces.
- **Adding a fence style moves the gate tiles in the palette.** Every painted gate would then turn
  into a fence post. This is latent.

## Fixed in this review (high)

### F1. The interior-wall layer deleted doors with their settings (editor, no undo)

- **Where:** `game/world/interior/interior_wall_layer.gd` `sync_doors` (the `node.free()` calls).
  It ran on every `changed` (each frame of a stroke) and in `_ready`.
- **What went wrong:** a door the layer had tagged was freed outside the editor's undo whenever its
  block stopped reading as that kind. That happened mid-stroke, on erasing one cell (the block's key
  moves to the next cell), on a repaint, or on a camera room moved off the block. Ctrl+Z restored the
  tiles, and the sync then made a fresh door with default settings. Adopted doors were freed too,
  though the doc says "a door placed by hand is never removed".
- **Reproduce (before the fix):** open `r3-pit-hall.tscn`, erase cell (26,1), then press Ctrl+Z.
  `hub-boss-door` (`door_id` `fatty-lair-boss-door`) is gone, replaced by `r3-pit-hall-boss-26-0`. The
  same on cell (12,14) in `r12-larder.tscn` loses the shutter's `fight_id`.
- **Fix:**
  - A door the layer makes remembers its settings as made (`painted_object_made` meta, a snapshot
    of its exported values and position). Only such a door, unchanged since, goes with its block,
    so repainting and erasing a fresh door still swap or remove it as before.
  - A door with any setting changed, and every adopted (hand-placed) door, is never removed. Its tag
    is taken off and a warning names it. When its block is painted again it is adopted back, with
    its settings.
- **Test:** `test_interior_walls.gd` `test_painting_a_closed_door_makes_its_object` now also checks
  three things:
  - a door whose `door_id` was set survives an erase and a repaint;
  - it is adopted again when its block comes back;
  - an adopted hand-placed door survives its block's erase.

### F2. The fence layer deleted divider gates; painted gates shared one record

- **Where:** `game/world/fences/fence_layer.gd` `sync_gates`;
  `game/scenes/objects/dungeon/divider-gate.tscn` (`gate_id = "divider-gate"`, no `map_id`).
- **What went wrong:**
  - Adopted gates got the same tag as gates the layer made, and every tagged gate whose cell was no
    longer a gate tile was `free()`d.
  - Every gate made by painting kept the template record ("", "divider-gate"). So one key or latch
    opened all of them, and a `map_record("")` entry ended up in saves.
- **Reproduce (before the fix):** open `c2-weigh-house.tscn`, erase cell (11,7), then press Ctrl+Z.
  `c2-east-door` comes back as `divider-gate-11-7` with `gate_id "divider-gate"`, and its plate no
  longer opens it.
- **Fix:**
  - The same rule as F1: only a gate the layer made and nobody changed goes with its tile. Others
    are untagged, warned about, and adopted again.
  - A gate the layer makes gets its own record: `gate_id` `<room>-gate-<x>-<y>` and the world's
    record id as `map_id` (`DungeonState.record_map_id`).
- **Test:** `test_fences.gd` `test_painted_gates_keep_their_settings_and_records`.

### F3. Fences did not collide in worlds without painted levels

- **Where:** `game/world/fences/fences.gd` `_build_collision`.
  - It puts the fence bodies on the `level 0` physics layer.
  - Only `Elevation.track` adds level bits to a body's mask, and a world with no `elevation` paint
    has no Elevation.
  - The player's mask (world, enemy, npc, water) and the enemies' masks have no level bit.
- **What went wrong:** in `c2-weigh-house`, `r5-eye-hall` and `r5-lower-hall` (fences painted, no
  levels) bodies walked through every post and rail. Only the gates' own bodies blocked.
- **Reproduce (before the fix):** `?map=fatty-lair-c2-weigh-house`, then walk north through the row-7
  divider at x ≈ 600.
- **Fix:** fences mounted on flat ground (no Elevation) put their level-0 bodies on the `world` layer,
  which every walker collides with. Worlds with levels are unchanged. FENCES.md now says so.
- **Test:** `test_fences.gd` `test_a_flat_worlds_fence_stops_a_body` moves a body into a flat
  world's fence.

### F4. Every room of Fatty's Lair showed its own banner, named from its world id

- **Where:** `game/shell/shell.gd` `_on_world_registered`.
  - Only chunks of one region shared a banner. Every `fatty-lair-*` world got
    `AreaTitles.area_name(<id>)`, the id title-cased.
- **Reproduce (before the fix):** walk from R1 into R2. The banner reads "Fatty Lair R2 Slab
  Steps", and walking back shows "Fatty Lair".
- **Fix:** the worlds of a dungeon of one world per room (their Dungeon node's `record_map_id`,
  `DungeonState.world_record_id`) are titled as one world, as a region's chunks are. The banner
  shows on the way in, named by the record id's title, not again from room to room. The Weaver
  Hollow's floors keep their own banners (their Dungeon has no `record_map_id`).
- **Test:** `test_shell.gd` `test_one_banner_for_the_rooms_of_a_dungeon_of_one_world_per_room`.

## High, not fixed (needs the owner)

- **Where:** `game/world/elevation/elevation.gd`.
  - `_build_room_faces` frees only the Sides' drawing.
  - `_side_shapes` keep the outdoor shapes, which `_sides_walkable` and `_with_sides` collide on:
    - convex corners rounded with radius 40, back corners 34, inner corners 20;
    - every edge wobbling by about ±4 units.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The upper slime cannot reach the drawn corner of its floor.
  - The lower slime slides into the drawn corner of a face.
  - In a painted room (R7), `body_fits` (landings, hops, Rolls) still uses the rounded shapes.
  - `test_every_lair_room_drops_in_front_of_its_faces_and_its_slabs_crack` skips corners on
    purpose.
- **Reproduce:** in `glow-moss-burrow`, walk a level-0 slime on the island (cells 12–14, 6–8)

- **Fix (same code everywhere):** make the corner radii and the edge wobble a per-style setting of
  the side builder, 0 for the interior styles, so the rooms' outline is the faces' square footprint.
  Or draw the faces from `_side_shapes`.

### H2. A painted bridge never holds the slime at its sides

- **Where:** `game/world/elevation/level_collision_layer.gd:119`. A passage opens every painted
  cell of its level within 16 units of all four of its sides, whether or not floor lies beyond.
- **What goes wrong:**
  - A rim painted along a slab's long side is opened. One painted further out leaves the slime's
    feet off the deck when it presses the rim, so the hazard catches it. Walking off a painted
    bridge's side is an instant fall instead of the outdoor rim hold and ledge drop.
  - In R7 the north floor's rim is painted inside the pit's first quarter row (y 384–400), inside the
    north passage's span. So it is also opened beside the bridge end: a 16-unit gap each side.
- **Reproduce (suspected, numbers from the data):** in R7 at level 0, stand at about (580, 360) and
  walk down. The slime leaves the slab's edge and falls. With the layer cleared, the floor rim
  holds it.
- **Fix:** open a painted cell only where, 16 units beyond the passage's edge, the painted elevation
  is floor of the passage's level. Document one rim convention: a top's own edge cells, as the lab
  paints them.
- **Not done here:** another session is editing R7 right now.

### H3. A shut divider gate leaves a hole on each side that the slime fits through

- **Where:**
  - The gate's `Doors` shape is 52 × 10, covering ±26 (`divider-gate.tscn`).
  - The gate cell draws no post or rail (`fences.gd:338` skips gate styles). The nearest collision
    is the neighbours' posts at ±57, which leaves a 31-unit gap each side.
  - The slime's body is 30 × 26. The art shows the same gaps (eye block 44 wide, posts 24 wide at
    ±64).
- **What goes wrong:** the Old Armoury's chest (gate at cell 12,6) and R7's key (gate at 3,7: its
  west side happens to be closed by a lone tile's stub) can be reached past a shut gate. Holding up
  and right slides the body along the post band into the hole.
- **Fix (art and code):** draw half-rails from each neighbour's post up to the block, and let the
  fence collision treat a gate cell as a link (bands to the block's edges), with the gate's body
  covering only the middle. FENCES.md:174 "point it away from the gate" is the advice that opens
  the hole; it should be "point it at the gate".

### H4. Pockets that can be walked around, and room edges that are not bevel faces

- **What the scenes have:**
  - Burr's cell is its own world, but the hub's world (`r3-pit-hall.tscn`) has no wall round the
    cell. Its interior-walls rim is a full 36 × 16 rectangle and it has no `walls` layer. Cells
    (2..4, 2..8) are open ground, hidden only by the mask.
  - The cell door's body covers x 128..256, leaving x 256..320 open at y ≈ 576, and the north ledge
    (rows 2–3) runs west into the cell area.
  - The same in R5's lower hall: the alcove's fieldstone wall exists only in `r5-key-alcove.tscn`.
    The lower hall's north edge has only a 3-cell divider stub.
- **Reproduce (from the data, not played):**
  - `?map=fatty-lair-r3-pit-hall`, then walk north from about (288, 700) past the door's right jamb.
    The slime stands beside Burr in the dark.
  - The `hub-to-cell` trigger still refuses, so Burr's keys are not skipped. But the collision does
    not match the drawing.
- **The directive:** these pockets' edges are fieldstone walls and fence dividers with doors, not
  bevel faces with the door in the wall (INTERIOR_WALLS.md lists them as exceptions). Since each is
  now a world of its own, each is a room edge.
- **Fix (scene edits, the owner's scenes, the plan's Q1 is still open):** paint each pocket's own
  full two-cell rim with doorway blocks and put the room doors on their outer edges. Paint the
  separating rim (or a `walls` line) into the parent worlds, as R1's rim goes round the store.

### H5. Adding a fence style moves the gate tiles (latent)

- **Where:** `scripts/art/build-fence-paint-tiles.py:63` places the gates at
  `len(STYLES) + index`.
- **What goes wrong:** appending a style as FENCES.md "Making the art" says moves the gates from
  (8,0)/(9,0) to (9,0)/(10,0). The six painted gate cells become the new style's post. Before F2,
  the next open of each room would also have deleted its gate.
- **Fix:** give the gates fixed columns (or their own row), and append new styles after them.

## Simpler or faster

- **Interior walls rebuild everything on every change.** Each `changed` (each frame of a stroke)
  frees and remakes hundreds of Sprite2Ds per room. Draw the pieces and the floor in one `_draw()`
  each (only the fronts need y-sorted nodes). Cache `_camera_rooms()` per build. `order.has(p)`
  (:413) is O(n²). `room_style_at` refloods while `_floors` is empty (O(cells²) from `room_faces`).
  Merge collision boxes into runs (120–426 boxes a room).
- **Leaf fitting is written four times** (`dungeon_door`, `shutter`, `plank_barricade`,
  `sliding_slab`), and `WALL_TURNS`/`SIDE_DEPTH` are duplicated. Write one
  `InteriorWalls.fit_leaf(...)`.
- **`sync_doors` and `sync_gates` are near copies.** Merge them into one helper with the made/adopted
  rule of F1/F2, the editor guard, unique ids and undo.
- **Rooms build Sides and Patches only to free them.** They also bake and load them in the cache
  (R7's is 165 KB). Build only the shapes in rooms. RoomCollision (hundreds of `set_cell`s) is built
  at every rebuild in the game but used by the editor only.
- **Two copies of the outdoor ledge and paint rules.** `_painted_ledge_below` is a reduced copy of
  `ledge_below`. Painted-cell rules are copied in `room_faces.gd:50`, `painted_level_at` and
  `Hazard._cell_on_floor`. Two rect mergers (`LevelCollisionLayer.merged`,
  `HazardWatch.merged_rects`).
- **The level-collision tools reach into Elevation's private state.** Fill sets `_painted_on = false`
  and never restores it; the overlay clears `_outlines`. Expose public automatic-outline queries.
- **HazardWatch does per-frame work that could be cached.** It calls `get_nodes_in_group` twice per
  step and allocates `deck_rects()` per deck per step.
- **Shared test helpers.** `_stand`, `_enter`/`_travel`, `_settled`, `_route`, `_walk_to` and
  `_action` are copied across 15 test files. They belong in `tests/lib/test_context.gd`.
